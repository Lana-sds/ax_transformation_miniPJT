## Question quality (choices)

- Each wrong choice must carry one plausible misconception that a developer could really hold, for example a fix that only removes the symptom, or a correct pattern applied in the wrong context. It must still be wrong according to the documents. Never write a choice that makes no sense.
- No cues. The correct choice must not repeat words from the stem that the wrong choices lack. Do not put absolute words (only, always, never) in the wrong choices alone. Do not make the correct choice noticeably longer or more detailed than the others; keep all choices about the same length and form.
- `explanation_ko`: one paragraph per choice, in choice order, saying why that choice is right or wrong. Support every judgment with a passage quoted from the documents.
